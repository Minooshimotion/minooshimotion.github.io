A bright yellow robot, reimagined with a quieter palette and a weathered finish. The goal was to give an existing character a sense of age while preserving its charm.

01 / The brief

Keep the charm.
Show the years.

The starting artwork was a cute robot with a vivid yellow body and bright, rounded eyes. The brief called for a character that felt old and worn, while remaining appealing.

That balance shaped the rework. The oversized head, small body, and expressive eyes already gave the robot its personality. I focused on changing its surface and color treatment so those familiar features could tell a different story.

The original robot was supplied artwork. My contribution was the character’s visual rework, rather than its original design.

02 / The rework

From bright yellow
to aged metal.

I began by choosing a palette that suggested age. Muted grays replaced the saturated yellow, with earthy, rust-colored accents bringing warmth to the edges.

I then worked on the worn surface treatment. Mottled textures and uneven shading made the head and body feel less polished. The darker eyes changed the expression without losing the character’s soft, rounded appeal.

03 / The result

A new mood.
A familiar silhouette.

The final rework keeps the proportions and friendly presence of the supplied character, but gives it a more aged appearance. Color and texture carry the change: the robot feels weathered, while its large eyes keep it approachable.

This project explores how much a character’s story can change through its finish, even when its underlying shape stays familiar.
